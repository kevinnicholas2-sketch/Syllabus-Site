Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

  1. Explain core artificial intelligence concepts, including machine learning, neural networks, and generative AI.
  2. Apply AI tools and techniques to solve practical, real-world problems.
  3. Critically evaluate AI outputs for accuracy, bias, and ethical impact.
  4. Communicate AI-driven findings and project results clearly to technical and non-technical audiences.
