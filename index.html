Grading

Your final grade is calculated from the components below. Weights total 100%.

Grade breakdown
Component Description Weight
Participation Attendance and active engagement in class: joining discussions, asking questions, and taking part in live coding demos and in-class exercises. 10%
Quizzes Three short quizzes (Weeks 4, 6, and 12) on lecture and reading concepts such as context, plan mode, Git workflows, and responsible use. Equally weighted. 15%
Assignments Three hands-on assignments (Weeks 2, 6, and 10) where you use Claude to build, change, and verify real code, then submit your work with a short write-up of your process. Equally weighted. 25%
Midterm Exam In-class exam in Week 8 covering Weeks 1–7: prompting, Claude Code, CLAUDE.md, plan mode and testing, Git workflows, and tools and MCP. 20%
Final Project Capstone project built with Claude Code, graded on the proposal (Week 11), the finished project, and the final presentation (Week 14). 30%
Total 100%
